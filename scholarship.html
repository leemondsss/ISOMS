Scholarship Programs

Explore the scholarships available at PHINMA University of Pangasinan. Select a program to see where to confirm its current eligibility, requirements, and application details.

1. PHINMA Scholarship (formerly Presidential Scholarship)

Benefits

  • 100% discount on tuition and miscellaneous fees for 4 or 5 years.
  • ₱4,000 monthly allowance for Honors or Top 10 Grade 12 students who passed the qualifying exam.

Maintenance

  • Complete 2 hours of service per day at the assigned department.
  • No grade below 2.0.
  • Maintain an average of 1.75 or above.
  • Service may also be required during summer if included in the curriculum.

Requirements

  • Scholarship acceptance letter from the Office of the President.
  • Enrollment Registration.

Details provided from the supplied scholarship reference. Confirm current terms with the University Scholarship Office.

2. Student Assistance Scholarship

Benefits

  • 100% off tuition (21 units only).

Eligibility

  • Must be in good class standing.
  • Complete 4 hours of service per day at the assigned department.
  • Must have passing grades.
  • Not applicable during summer.

Requirements

  • Should pass the examination and interview.

Details provided from the supplied scholarship reference. Confirm current terms with the University Scholarship Office.
